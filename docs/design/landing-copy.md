# Landing Copy

## Goal
The landing page should explain Backbit in a few seconds.
It must communicate the product, the value, and the difference.

## Core pitch
Backbit is a mobile-first game tracker that helps you organize your backlog, follow your playthroughs, and decide what to play next with more context.

## Supporting pitch
It combines backlog management, ratings, reviews, session logging, and discovery into one focused experience that feels more like a personal game journal than a generic list app.

## What the landing must say
- Backbit helps you manage games you want to play.
- Backbit keeps your current playthroughs visible.
- Backbit helps you finally deal with backlog overload.
- Backbit gives you a cleaner way to discover the next game.

## Hero copy direction
Headline idea:
"Your backlog, finally playable."

Subheadline idea:
"A mobile-first game tracker for backlog, play sessions, reviews, and smarter discovery."

## Section ideas
- What Backbit is.
- How the board works.
- Why discovery feels different.
- Why it is built for mobile.
- Why it is not just another game list.

## Copy tone
The tone should be confident and editorial.
It should feel like product messaging, not marketing noise.
